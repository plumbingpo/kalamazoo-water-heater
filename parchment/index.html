How Parchment Homeowners Can Extend Water Heater Life

Routine maintenance makes all the difference between a water heater that lasts and one that floods the garage in year seven. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: an annual sediment flush, an anode inspection every few years, a yearly test of the T&P valve, a temperature setting around 120 degrees, and yearly descaling for any tankless unit on hard water, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Take a look at the age of your water heater. The rating plate on the tank usually encodes the manufacture date, and that date says a lot about what comes next. Most tank models reach 8 to 12 years, heat pump units 10 to 15, and regularly descaled tankless units around 20. If your heater is past that range and also shows rusty water, popping sounds, or dampness under the tank, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency replacement after a leak.

Left alone, a small problem tends to grow. Take a spent anode rod: swapping it out is a short, affordable repair, but once it's gone, the water begins corroding the tank itself, and suddenly a minor fix becomes a full replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and reach out at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and prevents a surprise cleanup.

What Goes Wrong With Local Water Heaters

Whatever type of water heater your Parchment home has, it tends to fail in a predictable way. Gas tank heaters tend to have trouble with thermocouples and flame sensors, sooty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric units burn out elements and thermostats, particularly when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless units throw error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition faults, and flow sensor trouble, and many of those trace back to skipped descaling. Heat pump water heaters depend on a clean filter and a free-flowing condensate line, and they struggle in cramped closets. We service all four types regularly and carry the parts to fix most problems on the first visit.
