Getting More Years From Your Water Heater in Grand Rapids

The best way to avoid a surprise leak is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners flush the tank once a year (more often with hard water), have the anode rod checked every three to five years, test the relief valve annually to make sure it isn't stuck, and keep the thermostat around 120 degrees, which limits scalding risk and cuts down on scale. Tankless owners should plan on descaling once a year in hard-water areas.

Take a look at the age of your water heater. The serial number on the label usually encodes the manufacture date, and that date tells you a lot. Most tank heaters reach 8 to 12 years, heat pump models 10 to 15, and well-maintained tankless units around 20. If your heater is beyond that point and is also showing discolored water, popping sounds, or moisture at the base, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than an emergency replacement once the tank fails.

If warning signs do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A dripping relief valve might point to excess pressure or a missing expansion tank, both modest fixes. The same drip ignored for months can keep stressing the tank until the tank itself fails, and a small repair becomes a full replacement and a cleanup. Typical Water Heater Issues in Grand Rapids

Most water heater failures in Grand Rapids announce themselves before they happen. Watch for a popping or rumbling sound while the tank heats, rust-colored hot water, a sulfur smell from the hot tap, a damp spot under the tank, or showers that run cold sooner every month. These are early signs of heavy sediment, a depleted anode, a weak element or burner, and early corrosion, and caught early, most of them mean a modest repair or a replacement on your schedule. Ignored, they end in a ruptured tank and a cleanup bill. Pay close attention to a relief valve that drips or discharges: it means the tank is seeing too much pressure or temperature, and it should be checked promptly.
